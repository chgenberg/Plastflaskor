# Nano Banana 2 — produktbilder per artikel

En prompt per produkt i katalogen. Samma bildfamilj som startsidans 10 bilder, men **produkten i fokus** (produktsida, 4:5-kort, cirkelbeskärning).

**Modell:** Nano Banana 2  
**Format:** 4:5 (portrait) — extra körning i 1:1 om du vill ha cirklar  
**Filnamn:** spara som slug, t.ex. `naturligt-mineralvatten-33cl.png`

Klistra in **LOOK**, sedan **SCEN**, sedan **NEGATIVES**.

---

## LOOK

Hyperrealistic commercial product photograph for a Scandinavian B2B beverage brand, 85mm lens, f/2.8, shallow but readable depth of field. Soft overcast daylight from camera-left, no beauty dish, no hard flash. Kodak Portra 400 color: warm paper, cool greens, restrained saturation. Palette locked to off-white #F5F5F7, birch, charcoal, forest pine, clear water, brushed aluminum, matte PET and uncoated paperboard. Photoreal condensation and paper-fiber labels. Product fills 60–70% of frame, three-quarter view, crop-friendly 4:5. No CGI plastic shine. Subtle film grain.

**Label lock (alla drycker/muggar):** no readable brand names. Use one shared abstract navy-and-off-white mark plus a thin color bar. Same mark family across the whole catalog so images sit together.

---

## NEGATIVES

no readable brand names, no fake Aqua Visibility wordmark, no barcodes in focus, no prices, no neon energy-drink clichés, no lightning bolts, no “ENERGY” lettering, no perfume bottle, no dropper, no glass wine-bottle silhouette for PET, no tropical plants, no marble bathroom, no stock-photo smile into camera, no collage, no watermark, no text overlay, no CGI.
