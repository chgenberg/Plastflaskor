# Nano Banana 2 — tomma 3D-baser till designmotorn

Kör dessa om du vill ha **rena produktfoton utan mark** som 3D-förhandsvisning i studion. Samma LOOK som övriga Nano Banana 2-bilder.

Spara som `studio-{slug}.png` i `public/Images/products/` (byt ut den nuvarande produktbilden om du vill att studion ska använda den nya basen).

Klistra in **LOOK**, sedan **SCEN**, sedan **NEGATIVES**.

---

## LOOK

Hyperrealistic commercial product photograph, 85mm lens, f/2.8. Soft overcast daylight from camera-left. Kodak Portra 400, palette #F5F5F7, birch, charcoal, forest pine, clear PET, matte aluminum, uncoated board. Full product visible with generous margin — cap, body and base all in frame, three-quarter view, 4:5. No label artwork, no logo, no mark, no color bar. Subtle film grain. No CGI.

## NEGATIVES

no logos, no brand marks, no readable text, no barcodes, no fake Aqua Visibility wordmark, no neon, no CGI plastic shine, no crop that cuts the cap or base, no watermark.
